Frequently asked questions

Is your furniture sustainable? ↑

Yes, we meticulously select materials with an eco-conscious mind, prioritizing options such as responsibly sourced wood, recycled materials, and low-impact production processes.

What is your return policy? ↓

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery on undamaged items in original condition. Please contact us at info@web.decotrendsmiami.com to initiate a return.

Do you offer customization options for furniture? ↓

Yes, many of our pieces are available in different upholstery options, finishes, and sizes. Contact our showroom team for custom orders.

What are your delivery times and charges? ↓

In-stock items ship within 1–3 business days and are typically delivered within 2–3 weeks. Delivery charges depend on location and order size.

Are there any discounts or promotions available on your furniture? ↓

Yes! We regularly run seasonal promotions and limited-time offers. Subscribe to our newsletter to be the first to know about exclusive deals.
